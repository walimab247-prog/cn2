Editorial Policy

Last updated: Aug 14, 2026

Scope

This policy describes how content on Crypto News is produced. It applies to all news articles, market overviews and explanatory material published on this site.

Sourcing

Articles are based on publicly available reporting from established industry outlets and on primary documents such as regulatory filings and official announcements. Each article includes a "Sources" section with links. We do not reproduce third-party text verbatim; all content is independently rewritten with added context and analysis.

Neutrality

We report in a neutral, informational style. We do not publish price predictions, trading signals, promises of profit or calls to buy or sell any asset. Where opinions of market participants are described, they are attributed and presented as views, not facts.

Accuracy and updates

Every article displays a publication date and an update date. When new information emerges or an error is identified, we amend the article and refresh the update date. Material corrections are acknowledged in the text of the article.

Corrections

Readers can report factual errors through the contact form. We review correction requests promptly and update content where the request is substantiated.

Separation from advertising

Editorial content is produced independently of any commercial relationships. Sponsored material, if any, is clearly labelled and governed by our Advertising Disclosure.
