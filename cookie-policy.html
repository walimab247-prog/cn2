What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser at the request of a website. Related technologies include local storage and session storage, which serve similar purposes.

What this site uses

Crypto News is a static informational site. It does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Only technically necessary browser storage may be used to support basic site functionality (for example, remembering an interface preference).

Third-party content

If embedded third-party content (such as external links you follow) sets cookies, those cookies are governed by the third party's own policies, not ours.

Managing cookies

You can view, block and delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies will not prevent you from reading content on this site.

Changes

If we introduce analytics or other cookie-based features in the future, this policy will be updated first and consent requested where legally required.
