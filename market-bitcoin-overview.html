Bitcoin Market Overview: Structure Over Price

Published: Aug 13, 2026  ·  Updated: Aug 14, 2026  ·  Author: Crypto News Editorial Desk

Market structure

Bitcoin trading is spread across regulated exchanges, ETF creation/redemption flows and offshore derivatives venues. Liquidity has gradually shifted toward regulated venues as institutional participation has grown, though offshore perpetual futures still account for a large share of price discovery.

What analysts watch

Commonly tracked indicators include ETF net flows, exchange balances, realized volatility and the share of supply held by long-term holders. None of these metrics predicts price on its own; they are used to characterize the state of the market rather than to time it.

Risks

Bitcoin remains a highly volatile asset. Liquidity can deteriorate rapidly during stress events, and leverage in derivatives markets amplifies moves in both directions. Historical patterns, including those around halving cycles, are not reliable guides to future behaviour.
